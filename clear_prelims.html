What is ClearPrelims?

ClearPrelims is a campaign that converts your preparation from “content chasing” into “marks maximization”. It is built around three ideas:

1) Askable Focus

Practice what can be asked

Not everything deserves equal attention. ClearPrelims prioritizes high-yield clusters that repeatedly show up in PYQs and their “nearby” variants.

Example: Instead of reading 5 sources for “Parliament”, you practice the question patterns UPSC actually uses.

2) Decision Skill

Improve accuracy under uncertainty

Prelims is not “write what you know”. It’s “choose correctly under time pressure”. We train elimination + risk discipline so you lose fewer marks to bad guesses.

Example: Two options look right? Learn the trap-pattern and the elimination habit.

3) Execution Loop

Consistency beats bursts

A simple weekly loop: Plan → Practice → Reflect (PIR). Your next week is shaped by your mistakes, not your mood.

Example: You don’t “revise randomly”. You revise the exact topics that cost you marks.

How ClearPrelims works (the loop)

The engine is simple: diagnose weak zones → practice askable patterns → reflect and tighten the next week.

Step 1
Diagnose

Start with PYQs / diagnostic sets. Identify “grey areas”.

Step 2
Target

Pick high-return topics. Avoid low-yield distractions.

Step 3
Practice

Train patterns + elimination. Track errors and attempt-type.

Step 4
Reflect

Weekly action list: revise these, practice these, stop doing these.

A simple promise

If you follow the loop for 6–8 weeks, you will notice: fewer silly mistakes, stronger elimination instincts, and a clearer sense of “what to do next”.

FAQ

Clear doubts fast. Then execute.

Is this only about PYQs?

PYQs are the base. ClearPrelims extends PYQs into “askable variants” and practices the same demand in multiple forms, so your learning generalizes instead of staying memorized.

Will this guarantee selection?

No honest person can guarantee that. We optimize for what we can control: focus, practice quality, elimination skill, and consistent execution—so your probability of clearing rises.

How is this different from reading more?

Reading increases input. ClearPrelims improves output: recall, decision-making, and mistakes reduction. You build a lean, exam-ready base instead of a bloated, hazy one.

Who should NOT do this?

If you’re still building fundamentals from zero and haven’t touched PYQs at all, start with basics first. ClearPrelims works best when you’re ready to practice and refine decision-making.
