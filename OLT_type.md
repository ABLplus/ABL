# 🧾 UPSC MCQ Option Layout Types (OLT)

This document categorizes the major types of option formats (Option Layout Types) used by UPSC in Civil Services Prelims MCQs. Each type includes a format description and an example.

| Code   | OLT Name                          | Format Description                                                                                                 | Example |
|--------|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| OLT-01 | Simple A/B/C/D                    | Four independent answer choices; choose the correct one                                                            | **Q:** Who was the first Chief Justice of India? <br> A. M. Patanjali Sastri <br> B. H. J. Kania <br> C. B. K. Mukherjea <br> D. Mehr Chand Mahajan |
| OLT-02 | Two Statements – 1/2/1&2/Neither   | Two statements given; identify which one(s) are correct                                                            | **Q:** Consider the following statements: <br> 1. The President can dissolve Lok Sabha. <br> 2. Rajya Sabha cannot be dissolved. <br><br>Which of the above is/are correct? <br> A. 1 only <br> B. 2 only <br> C. Both 1 and 2 <br> D. Neither 1 nor 2 |
| OLT-03 | Multiple Statements – Combo Codes | Three or more statements given; choose the correct combination                                                     | **Q:** Which of the following are Fundamental Duties? <br> 1. To protect the environment <br> 2. To promote scientific temper <br> 3. To safeguard public property <br><br> A. 1 and 2 only <br> B. 1 and 3 only <br> C. 2 and 3 only <br> D. 1, 2 and 3 |
| OLT-04 | Match the Following                | Two columns (List I and List II); match items and choose the correct code                                          | **Q:** Match List I with List II: <br><br> A. Article 14 — 1. Equality before law <br> B. Article 19 — 2. Freedom of speech <br> C. Article 21 — 3. Right to life <br> D. Article 32 — 4. Constitutional remedies <br><br> Select the correct code: <br> A. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4 <br> B. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3 <br> C. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2 <br> D. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1 |
| OLT-05 | Only N Pair(s) Correct             | Given 3 or 4 pairs; pick how many are correctly matched                                                            | **Q:** Consider the following pairs: <br><br> River — Origin <br> 1. Godavari — Nasik <br> 2. Narmada — Amarkantak <br> 3. Ganga — Yamunotri <br><br>Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched? <br> A. Only 1 <br> B. Only 2 <br> C. 1 and 2 only <br> D. All three |
| OLT-06 | Assertion and Reason (A, R)        | A statement (Assertion) and a Reason are given; determine correctness and whether R explains A                     | **Q:** Assertion (A): Deforestation leads to soil erosion. <br> Reason (R): Roots of trees hold soil in place. <br><br> A. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A <br> B. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A <br> C. A is true, R is false <br> D. A is false, R is true |
| OLT-07 | Dual Explanation Evaluation        | One main statement (I) followed by two possible explanations (II and III); assess correctness and explanatory value | **Q:** Consider the following statements: <br><br> Statement I: Activated carbon is useful in removing pollutants. <br> Statement II: It has a large surface area for adsorbing heavy metals. <br> Statement III: It can be synthesized from environmental waste. <br><br> Which one of the following is correct? <br> A. Both II and III are correct and explain I <br> B. Both II and III are correct but only one explains I <br> C. Only one is correct and explains I <br> D. Neither II nor III is correct |
| OLT-08 | Statement I–II Explanation Check   | Two factual statements given (I and II); check if each is correct and whether II explains I                        | **Q:** Consider the following statements: <br><br> Statement I: Income from allied agri-activities is exempt from tax. <br> Statement II: Income from rural agri land is exempt under the IT Act, 1961. <br><br> Which of the following is correct? <br> A. Both I and II are correct and II explains I <br> B. Both I and II are correct but II does not explain I <br> C. I is correct, II is incorrect <br> D. I is incorrect, II is correct |
| OLT-09 | List/Item Codes                    | 4–6 items given; pick the correct code (usually used in Science/Tech or Environment)                              | **Q:** Which of the following are bio-remediators? <br> 1. Blue green algae <br> 2. Fungi <br> 3. Earthworms <br> 4. Bacteria <br><br> A. 1, 2 and 3 only <br> B. 1, 3 and 4 only <br> C. 2, 3 and 4 only <br> D. 1, 2, 3 and 4 |
| OLT-10 | Visual/Map/Image Based             | Question includes a visual (map/image/diagram); options are based on interpretation                               | **Q:** The shaded region on the map shows: <br> A. Khasi Hills <br> B. Nilgiri Hills <br> C. Aravalli Range <br> D. Vindhya Range |
